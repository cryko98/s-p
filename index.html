Lore · Chapter 02

Before Bikini Bottom, there was Bob the Sponge

  1. Dana Point, California

    Stephen Hillenburg taught marine science. To show his students what lived in the tide pools, he drew a comic: The Intertidal Zone.

  2. Meet the guide

    One of its guides was Bob the Sponge. He wore sunglasses. He didn’t even have arms.

  3. Years later…

    Hillenburg drew him in the shape of a kitchen sponge, and the character clicked.

  4. May 1, 1999 · TV debut

    SpongeBob walks up to the Krusty Krab to ask for his dream job, then loses his nerve. Patrick talks him into going inside.

    Before any of us knew SpongeBob could cook, Patrick already believed in him.

In memory

Stephen Hillenburg

1961 – 2018

In March 2017, Stephen shared that he had ALS. He told fans he wanted to keep working on SpongeBob and the other things he loved for as long as he could. He died on November 26, 2018, at age 57.

Fans remembered him in a way that could only have come from Bikini Bottom.

1.2M+ people signed a petition asking for “Sweet Victory,” the song from Band Geeks, to be played at the Super Bowl in his honor.
